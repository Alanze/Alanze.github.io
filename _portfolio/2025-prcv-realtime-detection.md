**Period:** Sep 2025 – Nov 2025 · **Lab:** IWIN-FINS, SJTU

Built and optimized a real-time object-detection stack for competition-oriented / PRCV-style scenarios:

- Trained and compared **YOLOv5**, **YOLOv8**, and **YOLO-Nano** backbones  
- Exported models to **ONNX** and deployed with **TensorRT** for low-latency inference  
- Designed an **ROI-focused** pipeline to improve accuracy on regions of interest  
- Achieved **150+ FPS** on target hardware while maintaining competitive **mAP**

**Stack:** PyTorch, YOLO, ONNX, TensorRT, GPU
